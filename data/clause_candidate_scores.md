# Phase 7 step 1 — clause-text candidate scores

Generated by `backend/scripts/score_clause_candidates.py` (build-time, stdlib only). Text is the Public.Resource.Org mirror on the Internet Archive — never a BIS publication. Only the edition MetrIQ's record cites is fetched; a missing edition is NOT_FOUND, never substituted. Nothing here is ingested.

Rules: SHIP needs ≥ 5 clean clauses, clause 1 = SCOPE with a readable body, ≤ 10% unreadable lines inside clause text, and the cover printing the cited number and year. A clean clause is a heading matched by `^\d+(\.\d+)*\s+[A-Z]` (or annex `^[A-Z]-\d+…`) that continues the numbering of the previous accepted heading, with a readable heading and ≤ 20% unreadable body lines. A page map of 0/N means `_page_numbers.json` exists but maps no leaf: every page would be "page not established".
